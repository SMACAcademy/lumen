## The content may contain mistakes

Lumen's explanations, simulations, numbers, questions and answers were prepared with the help of software tools, including AI, and checked against NCERT textbooks. Even so, they may contain errors, omissions or simplifications. Simulations are models: they leave out effects that real experiments include, and they round numbers.

**Always check what you learn against your NCERT textbook, your teacher, and the official syllabus and notices of your board or exam.** Where Lumen and your textbook disagree, follow the textbook and [tell us](#found-a-mistake).

## Found a mistake?

We want to fix it, and we fix mistakes quickly. A wrong explanation is worse than a missing one.

1. **Report it:** [open a correction issue](https://github.com/SMACAcademy/lumen/issues/new?template=correction.yml). Say where it is, what it says, what it should say, and your evidence (the NCERT chapter, page, table or another reliable source).
2. **Or fix it yourself:** open a pull request with the change and the same evidence in its description. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Every change is reviewed and must pass the automated checks before it is merged.

Corrections are accepted on the strength of the evidence, and the fix goes live on the site once it is merged.

## Privacy

Lumen has no accounts, no server and no tracking. Your name, progress and settings stay in your own browser and are never sent anywhere.
